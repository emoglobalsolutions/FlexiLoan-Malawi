HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

Is this a real lending service?

This downloadable version is a website prototype. It does not approve loans, hold customer accounts, or transfer money.

How do I qualify for a loan?

Eligibility must be determined by the actual lender and explained in its approved loan policy. Do not submit sensitive information to this demo.

Can I repay with Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba?

The interface can be extended to support mobile money, but live payments require approved provider access, backend verification, and secure transaction handling.

Are the loan limits and calculator results guaranteed?

No. All displayed limits and calculator results are illustrative placeholders and must be replaced with verified terms before launch.
